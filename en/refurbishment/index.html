What does it cost?

A fixed price based on your home – not a loose standard figure.

The price of a refurbishment depends on the size of the home, the condition of walls, ceilings and woodwork, the floors' needs and the requirements in the lease. That's why we start with an inspection and provide one written quote.

You can see every item and freely choose what you handle yourself and what Homie takes care of. Homie is typically 20–30% cheaper than the prices tenants experience via their landlord, though the actual saving always depends on the home and the landlord's statement.

Documentation and guarantee

Documented quality from inspection to handover.

We document the home's condition with photos and normally carry out our own quality check 1–3 days before the handover inspection. Where agreed, we attend the inspection and note relevant remarks.

The guarantee covers all work included in the accepted quote. Your landlord remains responsible for the final deposit statement and may offset other matters not covered by the quote.

FAQ

The most common questions.

How long does a refurbishment take?

It depends on the size of the home and the scope of work. The timeline is stated in the quote and agreed around your handover inspection.

What if the landlord rejects something?

If the remark concerns work in the accepted quote, we handle it per the guarantee terms. Other matters can be assessed separately.

Does Homie attend the inspection?

Yes, when attending the handover inspection is part of the agreed solution.
