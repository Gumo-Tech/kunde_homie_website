Refreshing worn floors

Light or full sanding – as needed.

A light sanding can be enough when wear is mainly on the surface. With deep scratches, discoloration or uneven treatment, a full sanding may be the right solution.

If the floor is very uneven or covered in heavy layers, planing may be needed before the finer sanding. We always assess the floor type and wear layer first.

FAQ

The most common questions.

Can all wood floors be sanded?

No. It depends on the floor type and the thickness of the wear layer, which we assess before the quote.

How quickly can the floor be used?

It depends on the chosen treatment. The specific drying and curing time is provided with the solution.

Is finishing included in the price?

Only when it's stated in the written quote. All included items are listed clearly.
