Nordic craftsmanship

A floor is more than a surface. It sets the tone for the whole room.

We help with more than the installation itself. Direction, centre line, grading, transitions and treatment are planned together, so the floor feels calm and coherent.

The result is a timeless floor that suits both classic apartments and modern, open kitchens. Solid herringbone can be sanded and re-finished several times, making it a long-term investment in your home.

More than herringbone

We of course also lay every other type of floor.

Herringbone is our specialty, but our floor team also helps with other flooring solutions. We advise on materials, subfloor, transitions and the finish that suits your home.
