Painting service across Zealand

Painting for homes, businesses and move-outs.

Homie carries out painting work in Copenhagen, North Zealand and the rest of Zealand. We help with everything from a simple refresh to complete painting as part of a move-out or renovation.

The quote describes precisely which surfaces and treatments are included, giving a clear expectation and a better basis for the final inspection.

Preparation matters

An even surface takes more than a paint roller.

We assess cracks, holes, stains, sheen and previous treatments before work begins. The agreed preparation is carried out so the paint gets an even, durable finish.

We aim to use responsible materials and paint with relevant eco-labels wherever the solution and surface allow.

Clear handover

Quality control and guarantee on the agreed work.

Once the work is finished, we quality-check the painting items listed in the quote. The guarantee covers all work in the accepted quote and follows the agreed guarantee terms.
