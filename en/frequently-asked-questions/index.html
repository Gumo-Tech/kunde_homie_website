First things first

How to get started.

Book a free inspection so we can see the home and give you a written quote. You don't need every document ready to get a quote, but a move-in report and photos can make our advice more precise.

FAQ

The most common questions.

I'm moving out – what happens?

We inspect the home, assess the necessary work and provide one combined quote. If you accept, we coordinate execution, documentation and the agreed handover.

What should I do first?

Book a free inspection. Have the names of all tenants ready, and send your move-in report, photos and the handover inspection notice when you have them.

Can I do some of the work myself?

Yes. You choose which items you handle yourself and which Homie takes care of. Julius is happy to advise on where it pays off best.

What does the guarantee cover?

The guarantee covers all work in the accepted quote. Your landlord still decides the final deposit statement.

Should I fix small things before the inspection?

No. Please leave the home as it is, so we can assess the job correctly together.
