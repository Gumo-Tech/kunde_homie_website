Our story

It started with an expensive move-out.

Homie was founded in late 2020 by people who had themselves experienced how overwhelming a move-out – and losing part of a deposit – can be. The idea was simple: tenants should have one professional partner who could create an overview before the work began.

Clear communication

Agreements, scope and changes should always be understandable to the customer.

Respect for the budget

A proper handover

Partnerships

Homie has worked with partners including LEJKA and DEAS. For us, a good partnership is about clear roles, quick access to relevant information and a shared focus on a proper process.
